Fine Art & Archival Editions

Hand-pulled prints on 300gsm Somerset Velvet cotton rag with deckled edges

CURATORIAL ANALYSIS

Each edition is printed under Chintwo’s direct atelier supervision using twelve-color archival pigment matrices on mould-made cotton paper with hand-torn deckled edges. Every print is individually titled, numbered, and embossed with the studio chop mark.

To preserve the utmost chromatic depth and archival permanence, our limited editions are produced strictly on 100% acid-free rag papers using museum-grade carbon and mineral pigments guaranteed lightfast for over two hundred years.
