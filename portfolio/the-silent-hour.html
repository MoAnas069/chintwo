The Silent Hour

Oil, carbon sumi ink, and crushed Badakhshan slate on hand-sized linen

CURATORIAL ANALYSIS

The Silent Hour explores the liminal threshold between twilight and absolute darkness. Painted across sixteen layering sessions in the East London atelier, the heavy textural impasto absorbs ambient gallery lighting, giving the silhouette of the central solitary figure an eerie, atmospheric presence that shifts as the viewer moves around the canvas.

By muting unnecessary narrative signifiers, Chintwo isolates the emotional resonance of solitude. The deep blacks are not flat pigments; they are compounded from pine soot and ground mineral slate, creating a tactile surface that breathes with ambient room light.

Dimensions 180 × 120 CM (70.8 × 47.2 IN)
Medium & Ground Oil, carbon sumi ink, and crushed Badakhshan slate on hand-sized linen
Current Provenance Private Collection, London
Authentication Chintwo Atelier Certificate of Authenticity
