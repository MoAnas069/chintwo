CURATORIAL ANALYSIS

A comprehensive record of solo presentations, collaborative museum installations, and international gallery expositions spanning London, Kyoto, Tokyo, and Paris. Includes catalog documentation, curatorial essays, and archival provenance records.

Over six years of continuous exhibition practice, Chintwo has developed an architectural approach to space curation, configuring natural light and gallery soundscapes to amplify the reflective, contemplative power of monochrome canvases.
